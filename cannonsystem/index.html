Quick Start

Playable black-powder artillery for Unreal Engine 5.8 (Win64).

What is inside

5 guns (/CannonSystem/CannonSystem/Blueprints)1-pounder falconet, 3-pounder, 12-pounder, 24-pounder, 13-inch mortar
ACannonPieceman, traverse, elevate, fire; recoil and wheel roll; muzzle flash, smoke and powder cloud; camera shake
ACannonShotreal ballistics (air drag that changes near the speed of sound); skips at shallow angles, buries in earth, drops off walls; the mortar bomb bursts after a fuse
UCannonGunnerComponentplayer control on Enhanced Input
ACannonTargetRespawnerbreakable Chaos targets: stones break off where shots land, a badly hit target collapses, and it rebuilds itself
UCannonShotResultSubsystemthe hit / miss result of every shot as a Blueprint event (OnShotResult); the demo HUD shows it
Demomap L_CannonRange, CannonDemoGameMode, CannonDemoPlayerController, CannonDemoPawn, CannonDemoHUD

Try it

  1. Install the plugin from Fab, then enable Cannon System in Edit > Plugins and restart. (Or put the CannonSystem folder in YourProject/Plugins/ and open the project.)
  2. Your project still opens its own map. In the Content Browser, open Settings and tick Show Plugin Content (and Show Engine Content if Fab installed the plugin to the engine).
  3. Open Plugins > CannonSystem Content > CannonSystem > Maps > L_CannonRange and press Play. The first time, shaders and effects compile for a minute or two: smoke may be missing on the first shots. The next Play looks right.
  4. Fly with WASD + mouse (E/Space up, Q/Ctrl down). Go to a gun.
KeyAction
Cman the gun / step off
Mouseaim (on the mortar, up/down sets the powder charge)
Left mousefire
Escstep off
Tbattery fire: every gun fires on its own, or stops
Rrebuild the targets

Use it in your game

  1. Add Cannon Gunner (UCannonGunnerComponent) to your PlayerController.
  2. Drag a gun Blueprint into your level.
  3. Keys: point the component's input slots at your own Input Actions / Mapping Contexts. The defaults are in /CannonSystem/CannonSystem/Input. C may clash with your crouch key.

Damage uses the engine's damage system: a shot applies point damage, a mortar bomb radial damage. The player who mans the gun is the instigator. Shots carry the actor tag Cannonball.

Main settings

  • Gun: RangeScale (shrinks the whole flight to fit a smaller map, same arc), elevation limits (MaxElevationSwingDeg, MaxDepressionSwingDeg), FireCooldown, RecoilDistance, bExplosiveShell + ShellFuseSeconds.
  • Shot (ACannonShot): MaxRicochetAngleDeg, MaxBounces, ImpactDamage, ExplosionDamage, ExplosionRadius.

Notes

  • Ranges are real: a 24-pounder reaches about 2 km and the mortar up to about 3.8 km. Give your map ground that far, or lower RangeScale.
  • Unreal Engine 5.8, Win64, single-player.
  • Uses the engine's Niagara and Enhanced Input plugins (enabled automatically) and Chaos Geometry Collections for the targets.
  • Documentation: https://4rev.github.io/rothenburg-series/cannonsystem/
